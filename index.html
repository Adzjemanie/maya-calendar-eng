Maya Calendar
Daily Message combines the day’s tone (1–13) with its day sign (20).
Maya Time Cycles
Tzolk’in (260 days)
The Tzolk’in combines two cycles: a tone (1–13) and a day sign (20). Together they create 260 unique days.
Haab’ (365 days)
The Haab’ is the solar calendar: 18 months of 20 days plus 5 transitional days called Wayeb’.
Long Count
The Long Count is written as baktun.katun.tun.uinal.kin to count days over long periods.
