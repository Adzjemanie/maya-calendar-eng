Maya Calendar
Maya Time Cycles
Tzolk’in (260 days)
The Tzolk’in is a sacred cycle of 260 days. Each day combines a tone (1–13) and a day sign (20). Together they create 260 unique day combinations.
Haab’ (365 days)
The Haab’ is the Maya solar calendar of 365 days. It consists of 18 months of 20 days plus 5 additional days called Wayeb’, a transitional period between years.
Long Count
The Long Count records the number of days since the Maya creation date. Dates are written as: baktun.katun.tun.uinal.kin.
Katun
A katun is a period of 7,200 days (about 20 years). Katuns are named after the Tzolk’in day Ajaw on which the katun ends.
Baktun
A baktun consists of 20 katun (144,000 days), which is about 394 years.

Completion, integration, transcendence. Close the cycle and harvest the lesson.
